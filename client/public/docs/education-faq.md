### FAQ

- **What is CROPT?** Settlement unit pegged 1:1 to USD on Cropto.
- **Where do prices come from?** Ukrainian CPT Odesa / CPT Paritet assessments (export vs processing).
- **How is margin set?** Percentage of notional based on time to expiry; premium can offset if enabled.
- **Can I lose more than the premium?** Buyers: at most the premium. Sellers: risk is collateralised via margin; margin calls protect counterparties.
- **Are these contracts on-chain?** Yes, options can be represented as NFTs and settled in CROPT.
