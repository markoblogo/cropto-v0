# Cropto Revival Roadmap

This roadmap frames Cropto as a revival-ready functional prototype inside the AMI ecosystem. It is not a commitment to launch a regulated trading venue without partner, legal and operational preparation.

## Now

- Preserve and polish the functional prototype.
- Align public positioning with AMI.
- Document implemented tokenization and settlement proofs.
- Connect Cropto narrative to 1D3X/SPIKE indices.
- Keep README, website copy and investor materials consistent.
- Identify legacy crypto-first wording in docs/locales and replace it with document-verification and settlement-infrastructure language.
- Maintain build/typecheck health.

## Next

- Consume live or demo index data from 1D3X/SPIKE.
- Prepare indexed trading simulation using local benchmark references.
- Define internal USD-linked settlement-unit architecture.
- Decide which flows require public-chain, permissioned-ledger, private-ledger or non-crypto accounting.
- Select legal/regulatory architecture for any pilot.
- Design partner pilot scope: market, commodity, participant type, settlement flow and reporting package.
- Harden demo data boundaries so users can clearly distinguish prototype activity from production obligations.
- Update localized public copy across English, Ukrainian, Spanish and Portuguese.

## Later

- Regulated partner-backed spot/options workflows.
- Document verification pilot with real counterparties and signed documents.
- Settlement and clearing integration with qualified partners.
- Index-linked risk instruments for local commodity markets.
- Logistics index instruments based on AMI benchmark infrastructure.
- Compliance-grade audit trail, permissions, monitoring and incident response.
- Investor/partner reporting package based on pilot evidence.

## Decision Gates

Before any production launch, owners should confirm:

- jurisdiction and regulatory perimeter;
- participant eligibility;
- clearing/custody/payment partners;
- index governance and publication rules;
- settlement-unit accounting treatment;
- dispute-resolution process;
- data retention and audit requirements;
- security review scope.
