# Broker Overview: Managing Client Relationships and Risk

## Story
Maria runs a commodity trading brokerage firm. She has 50 farmer clients hedging crops, 20 institutional traders, and 5 liquidity providers. Her goal is to grow the business while managing risk and ensuring all parties benefit from the ecosystem.

## Business Model Overview
As a broker in the Cropto ecosystem, Maria earns fees from matching trades, providing liquidity, and managing risk. She connects farmers, traders, and institutions while earning from transaction fees and spread capture.

## Daily Operations

### 1. Client Onboarding
**Partners & Contracts Section**:
- Register new farmer clients (hedging needs)
- Onboard institutional traders (speculative positions)
- Connect liquidity providers (market making)
- Set fee-sharing agreements (typically 10-20% of platform fees)

### 2. Fee Management
**Platform Fees Dashboard**:
- Track matching fees (0.5-2% per trade)
- Monitor settlement fees (collected on exercised options)
- View exercise fees (collected on option settlements)
- Calculate monthly payouts to partners

### 3. Risk Monitoring
**Risk Dashboard**:
- Monitor total margin utilization across all clients
- Track margin call alerts (prevent liquidation cascades)
- View concentration risk by commodity and client type
- Set risk limits and alerts

### 4. Market Making
**Liquidity Management**:
- Provide quotes on both sides of major spreads
- Earn bid-ask spreads on active contracts
- Use **Forward Spreads** for structured trades
- Participate in **Cross-Commodity Arbitrage**

## Revenue Streams

### Transaction Fees
- **Matching Fees**: 0.5-2% on each trade executed
- **Settlement Fees**: Fixed amount per ton settled
- **Exercise Fees**: Percentage of option payout

### Spread Capture
- **Calendar Spreads**: Buy cheap, sell expensive time periods
- **Cross-Commodity**: Exploit price inefficiencies between related commodities
- **Index Arbitrage**: Between spot and futures prices

### Partnership Fees
- **Referral Fees**: 10-20% from client platform fees
- **Custody Fees**: For collateral management services
- **Prime Brokerage**: Fee for enhanced margin terms

## Risk Management Framework

### Margin Management
**Key Metrics to Monitor**:
- Total collateral locked: $2.5M across all clients
- Margin utilization: Currently 65% (target <80%)
- Largest single position: 2000 tons corn (limit 5000 tons)
- Margin call frequency: 3 calls this month (target <5/month)

### Client Risk Profile
**Farmer Clients** (80% of volume):
- Conservative hedging strategies
- Low default risk, seasonal patterns
- Primary revenue: matching fees

**Institutional Traders** (15% of volume):
- Higher risk, leveraged positions
- Sophisticated strategies (spreads, options)
- Higher fee capture potential

**Liquidity Providers** (5% of volume):
- Market making, arbitrage
- Low risk, high volume
- Fee sharing arrangements

### Systemic Risk Controls
- **Position Limits**: Max 10% of open interest per client
- **Concentration Limits**: Max 25% exposure per commodity
- **Margin Requirements**: Dynamic based on volatility
- **Circuit Breakers**: Automatic halts on extreme moves
