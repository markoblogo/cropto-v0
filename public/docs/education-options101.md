### Options 101

- **CALL** gives the buyer the right to benefit if price goes **above** strike.
- **PUT** gives the buyer the right to benefit if price goes **below** strike.
- **Strike ($/t):** target price for the payoff.
- **Quantity (t):** contract size.
- **Premium (CROPT/t):** upfront cost paid by buyer, received by seller.
- **Expiry:** last day the option can be exercised; linked to the index window.
- Payoff is intrinsic value at expiry; premium is sunk cost.
