# What is Cropto and why does it exist?

Cropto is an educational pilot for pricing, hedging and trading Ukrainian grain using index-linked contracts.
Instead of quoting “random” FOB or CPT prices, Cropto focuses on a small, transparent set of indices such as Corn, Feed Wheat and Wheat 11.5%.

The goal is simple:

- give **farmers** a way to lock in a minimum price for future harvests;
- give **traders** and **exporters** a cleaner way to quote and manage risk on grain flows;
- give **processors** tools to hedge crush margin and input costs.

Under the hood the platform combines:

- **spot indices** sourced from public market data and Telegram feeds;
- **spot tokens** that represent exposure to these indices;
- **options** on indices, settled in CROPT (a dollar-denominated internal unit).

The current version runs on **Polygon Amoy testnet** with demo users and test balances so partners can safely explore how the workflows feel before moving anything to production.
